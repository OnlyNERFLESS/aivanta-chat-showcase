# Changelog

## Free 1.4.0 and Pro 1.0.0: channels, languages, Free and Pro

- Contact channels: WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Discord, GitHub, email and a custom link, each with its brand icon. Choose the contact buttons and up to three for the bottom bar.
- Free and Pro: AI answers, ready answers, products and categories and button animations move to the new Aivanta Chat Pro add-on, unlocked by a license key checked offline.
- Persian and Arabic translations with right-to-left layout and the Vazirmatn font; every other WordPress language through translation.
- Default settings follow a change of site language.
- An optional "Powered by" link, off until the site owner switches it on.
- The free plugin passes WordPress's Plugin Check with no errors.

## 1.3.1: Aivanta Chat

- New name and logo, in the dashboard menu, page headers and License page.
- A clean default template: general forms, starter answers in English and Arabic, and the new logo as the default chat icon.
- Two copies of the plugin active at once no longer crash the site; the second one steps aside and says so.

## 1.3.0: Phones and Custom CSS

- On phones the closed chat button now sits under the theme's menu and pop-ups instead of on top of them. The open chat is always on top.
- The chat stays fixed to the screen even when a theme applies effects to the whole page.
- Phone settings moved into Appearance, with a live phone preview.
- Custom CSS box with a selector guide, tooltips and one-click examples.

## 1.2.10: Header icon

- The small icon at the top of the open chat follows the button's shape, border and image size.

## 1.2.9: Shapes and a new Appearance page

- Button shapes: circle with border, plain circle, or the image on its own.
- Appearance reorganised into sections, with live demo cards, sliders and a visual corner picker.

## 1.2.8: Animation templates

- Five button animations: Spinning border, Floating, Ripple rings, Comet and Hello wave.
- Sliders for button size, image size and border thickness, plus two border colours.
- Live preview of the button on a light and a dark page.

## 1.2.7: A steadier button

- The icon fills its border with no gap, and only the border moves.

## 1.2.6: Fits into WordPress

- The admin screens follow WordPress's own look and the admin colour scheme.
- Fixed the chat icon picker, which could not open the media library.
- New dashboard menu icon.

## 1.2.0 to 1.2.5

- Form builder, ready answers, optional AI answers, request dashboard, WhatsApp hand-off and the security model described in the README.
