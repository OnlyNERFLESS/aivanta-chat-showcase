# Changelog

## Free 2.0.0 and Pro 2.0.0: Aluvo Chat

- **The plugin is now Aluvo Chat** (formerly Aivanta Chat), a name of its own on WordPress.org. Its address is aluvo-chat.
- **Custom CSS moves to Pro**, with its guide to every selector. WordPress.org does not allow free plugins to store custom CSS; every design option stays free.

## Free 1.8.0 and Pro 1.4.0: five animations free

- **Five button animations are free:** Spinning border, Floating, Ripple rings, Comet and Hello wave.
- **Pro** keeps Heartbeat, Breathing glow, Attention bounce, Orbit and Shine sweep, the animation colour and the phone animation, now carried entirely by the add-on. On Free they show as cards with a looping preview.
- **Category and product steps** are open to every form; with no products listed the chat skips them.
- **Get Pro** links go to digidev.pro/aluvo-chat.

## Free 1.7.0 and Pro 1.3.0: chat window preview, Pro previews

- **Live chat window preview** under Appearance, beside the button and phone previews. It follows every colour and gradient as you change it.
- **Preview Pro on Free:** pick any Pro animation, or a phone animation, to see it play. It is not saved without Pro.
- **Pro screens** (Products, Answers, AI answers) show a picture of how they look with Pro.
- **New section bar** on Customize and Forms, with an icon for each section.

## Free 1.6.0 and Pro 1.2.0: border types, inside gradient, animations for phones

- **Border types:** gradient, solid, dashed, dotted, double and neon, each with a live demo card.
- **Inside the button:** a two-colour gradient, beside the theme colour, your own colour and transparent.
- **Animation colour (Pro):** ripples, glow, orbit, comet spark and shine can take their own colour instead of the border's.
- **Phones (Pro):** their own animation, or none, separate from computers.
- **Five new animations (Pro):** Heartbeat, Breathing glow, Attention bounce, Orbit and Shine sweep. Ten in all.
- Fixed: Floating no longer shows a dark edge under the button as it rises.

## Free 1.5.0 and Pro 1.1.0: a new forms page, products, answers and gradients

- **Forms redesigned.** Three columns: your forms, the open form, and a live preview of it in the chat. New forms start from a template (quote, appointment, question, callback, support or blank), each form's icon is picked from a gallery, steps come from a visual gallery, and a short guide explains how it all fits together.
- **Products & categories and Answers moved under Forms.** Old links redirect.
- **Products (Pro):** your own products or live links to WooCommerce products. New fields: tags, price, offer, button text and link, and more details. Product cards in the chat show all of them.
- **Answers (Pro):** each answer reads like the chat, trigger words are chips, and trigger words in other languages have their own list.
- **Gradient themes:** Aurora, Ocean, Sunrise, Violet and Electric, plus a custom two-colour gradient with an angle. Both colours are checked for readable white text on save.
- **Inside the button:** follow the theme, choose a colour, or make it transparent.
- Language names removed from settings and descriptions; the plugin simply follows the WordPress language.
- Fixed: a quote mark typed into a form no longer breaks saving.

## Free 1.4.0 and Pro 1.0.0: channels, languages, Free and Pro

- Contact channels: WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Discord, GitHub, email and a custom link, each with its brand icon. Choose the contact buttons and up to three for the bottom bar.
- Free and Pro: AI answers, ready answers, products and categories and button animations move to the new Aluvo Chat Pro add-on, unlocked by a license key checked offline.
- Translations with right-to-left layout; every WordPress language through translation.
- Default settings follow a change of site language.
- An optional "Powered by" link, off until the site owner switches it on.
- The free plugin passes WordPress's Plugin Check with no errors.

## 1.3.1: Aluvo Chat

- New name and logo, in the dashboard menu, page headers and License page.
- A clean default template: general forms, starter answers, and the new logo as the default chat icon.
- Two copies of the plugin active at once no longer crash the site; the second one steps aside and says so.

## 1.3.0: Phones and Custom CSS

- On phones the closed chat button now sits under the theme's menu and pop-ups instead of on top of them. The open chat is always on top.
- The chat stays fixed to the screen even when a theme applies effects to the whole page.
- Phone settings moved into Appearance, with a live phone preview.
- Custom CSS box with a selector guide, tooltips and one-click examples.

## 1.2.10: Header icon

- The small icon at the top of the open chat follows the button's shape, border and image size.

## 1.2.9: Shapes and a new Appearance page

- Button shapes: circle with border, plain circle, or the image on its own.
- Appearance reorganised into sections, with live demo cards, sliders and a visual corner picker.

## 1.2.8: Animation templates

- Five button animations: Spinning border, Floating, Ripple rings, Comet and Hello wave.
- Sliders for button size, image size and border thickness, plus two border colours.
- Live preview of the button on a light and a dark page.

## 1.2.7: A steadier button

- The icon fills its border with no gap, and only the border moves.

## 1.2.6: Fits into WordPress

- The admin screens follow WordPress's own look and the admin colour scheme.
- Fixed the chat icon picker, which could not open the media library.
- New dashboard menu icon.

## 1.2.0 to 1.2.5

- Form builder, ready answers, optional AI answers, request dashboard, WhatsApp hand-off and the security model described in the README.
